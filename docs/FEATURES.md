# 🌟 ReDessIo Features & Capabilities

ReDessIo is a next-generation **Autonomous Spatial Design & Procurement Agent Harness** that bridges natural language creative intent with real-time architectural drafting, spatial mathematics validation, and sensitive procurement authorization.

---

## 📑 Feature Breakdown

### 1. 🏛️ 2D Architectural CAD Floor Plan with Realistic Furniture Symbols
- **High-Fidelity Drafting Symbols**:
  - **King & Queen Platform Beds**: Double contoured pillows with seam stitching, duvet quilt fold lines, and solid architectural headboards.
  - **Sectional Sofas & Couches**: Individual cushion seam partitions, structured armrests, and contoured backrest bars.
  - **Work Desks & Tables**: Ergonomic desktops with monitor screen footprints and cable grommets.
  - **Coffee Tables**: Double-bordered tops with chamfered inner wood inlay lines.
  - **Nightstands & Bedside Tables**: Flanked along bed boundaries with ambient circular lamp fixtures.
  - **Modular Bookshelves & Dressers**: Vertical bay dividers and drawer compartments.
- **Walkway Circulation Corridors**:
  - Dotted curved pathways (`stroke-dasharray="4 4"`) tracing 36" clearance routes from the main entryway around furniture.
- **Structural Wall & Window Envelopes**:
  - Thick outer wall cavities with glass pane window cutouts on North and West elevations.

---

### 2. 🤖 Live AI Spatial Design Copilot (GPT-OSS-120B)
- **Natural Language Layout Modification**:
  - Instruct the AI with natural commands such as:
    - `"move sofa towards wall"`
    - `"move bed to north wall"`
    - `"rotate seating 90 degrees"`
    - `"clear walkway from door"`
    - `"center layout with 360 degree walkway"`
- **Semantic Item Isolation**:
  - Targets the exact referenced furniture piece while preserving the rest of the room layout.
- **Real-Time Architectural Rationale**:
  - Returns concise architectural justifications powered by `openai/gpt-oss-120b` via Groq.

---

### 3. ⛶ Fullscreen Studio Mode
- **Side-by-Side Co-Design Environment**:
  - **Left / Center**: Full-scale 92vh high-definition 2D CAD architectural canvas with zoom, pan, and live spatial metrics.
  - **Right Sidebar**: Integrated AI Design Copilot Chat for real-time prompt-driven manipulation.
  - One-click `[ ⛶ Fullscreen ]` button and `ESC` hotkey dismiss.

  - 1.5-second continuous gesture preventing accidental financial authorization.

  - Generates immutable session hashes and audit logs.
